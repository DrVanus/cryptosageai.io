Features

Everything You Need to Trade Smarter

Portfolio management, AI predictions, trading bots, whale tracking, technical analysis, DeFi, and more — one app for your entire crypto workflow.

Portfolio Dashboard

Track your entire portfolio with allocation pie charts, performance graphs, P&L tracking, and AI-powered rebalancing suggestions. Import via CSV or connect exchanges directly.

AI Predictions & Insights

Get AI-powered price predictions for any token and timeframe. Coin insights, portfolio analysis, Fear & Greed AI observations, and price movement explainers — all scored by accuracy.

Paper Trading & Bots

Practice with $100,000 in virtual funds — zero risk, live market prices. Place market, limit, and stop orders. Build DCA, Grid, and Signal bots. Backtest strategies and browse the marketplace to copy top performers.

Watchlist & Market Movers

Monitor tokens with real-time prices, sparkline charts, and 1H/24H/7D performance. See trending coins, top gainers, losers, and full order book depth charts.

AI Chat Assistant

Ask anything in plain English — "What are the top gainers today?" Get instant, data-driven answers. Attach images, configure AI trades, and access full conversation history.

Whale Tracking & Smart Money

Monitor whale wallet activity in real time. Track smart money signals, watch specific wallets, and get alerts when big players move — so you're never caught off guard.

Technical Analysis

Full charting with RSI, MACD, ATR, Stochastic, OBV, and MFI indicators. Order book heatmaps, treemap visualizations, and volume analysis built in.

DeFi, NFTs & Hardware Wallets

Track DeFi positions, browse your NFT gallery, and connect Ledger or Trezor hardware wallets. Multi-chain support with WalletConnect integration.

Smart Alerts & Notifications

Set price alerts, whale alerts, and AI-powered market alerts. Get notified on Discord, Telegram, Twitter, and YouTube — wherever you are.

Tax Reports & Compliance

Generate tax reports with up to unlimited transactions. Tax-loss harvesting suggestions, manual entry support, and multi-currency compliance.

Social & Leaderboard

Create your profile, compete on leaderboards, share strategies, and copy trade from top performers in the community marketplace.

iOS Widgets

Portfolio value, Fear & Greed index, and price ticker widgets for your home screen. Glanceable market data without opening the app.
